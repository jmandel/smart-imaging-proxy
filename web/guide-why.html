Why imaging interoperability matters

Patients carry their records between health systems, but rarely their images. SMART Imaging lets any app a patient chooses fetch both, with one sign-in and the health system's own rules.

1 What it makes possible

  • A patient opens their scans in an app of their choice, next to their reports.
  • A parent or caregiver reaches what they're allowed to, and no more.
  • A referral service finds the one study it needs, not the whole record.
  • Images stay where they are. Nothing is copied ahead of time.

3 What it takes

  • A health system adds an imaging FHIR server and a DICOMweb endpoint behind the sign-in it already has for SMART apps.
  • An app uses the SMART App Launch it may already support, asking for imaging too.
