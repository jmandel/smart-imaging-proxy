Your sandboxes

A sandbox is a private copy of a SMART Imaging health system, with sample patients, studies and reports. Everything you do in one stays there until you delete it, and you can share one by its link.

    Someone sent you a link to theirs? Just open it.

    One sign-in, for a patient's chart and their images

    This app connects to a health system the way any SMART app would, using SMART Imaging Access. Choose a server, sign in, then watch every step of the protocol.

    Connect to

    Building a SMART app? Connect it to this sandbox.

    1. DiscoverFrom one FHIR address, find the sign-in page and the imaging server.
    2. AuthorizeSign in and approve this app for one patient's records.
    3. ReadUse the same token for clinical data and the list of studies.
    4. ViewRetrieve DICOM images and decode them here in the browser.
    Add or change servers

    A server needs a client registered with this sandbox's redirect URI:

    Each needs label, iss and clientId. scope is optional, and imaging names the imaging FHIR server when discovery doesn't. For a confidential app add "auth":"private_key_jwt" and jwk, a private ES256 key. For a backend service add "mode":"backend", jwk and patient. Keys saved here stay in this browser, so use them only for testing.
