One sign-in, for a patient's chart and their images

This app connects to a health system the way any SMART app would, using SMART Imaging Access. Choose a server, sign in, then watch every step of the protocol.

Connect to

  1. DiscoverFrom one FHIR address, find the sign-in page and the imaging server.
  2. AuthorizeSign in and approve this app for one patient's records.
  3. ReadUse the same token for clinical data and the list of studies.
  4. ViewRetrieve DICOM images and decode them here in the browser.
