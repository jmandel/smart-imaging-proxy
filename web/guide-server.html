Test your imaging server

Connect our viewer, a known-good SMART Imaging app, to your server. When something fails, you'll know it's the server, not the app.

1 What your server needs

  • CORS for on discovery, the token endpoint, FHIR searches and image retrieval. The viewer runs in the browser.
  • Discovery that names SMART Imaging by capability: smart-imaging-access with the app-launch capability, at the top level or on an entry in associated_endpoints.
  • ImagingStudy search by patient, with _include=ImagingStudy:endpoint and next links.
  • WADO-RS at each study's Endpoint.address, accepting the token, and 503 with Retry-After while images are prepared.
