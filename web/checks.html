Conformance checks

Each check acts out one situation the SMART Imaging Access guide covers. It runs against this sandbox's servers, as an app, an attacker or operations would act, and says whether the servers did what the guide asks.

Each check runs in a new sandbox of its own, so nothing here changes yours. Faults such as the decision service going down are set and cleared through that sandbox's management API.
