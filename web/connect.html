How to connect

  • Apps use PKCE (S256). Confidential apps also send a private_key_jwt assertion signed with ES256.
  • Backend services use client_credentials, a signed assertion, and system/ImagingStudy.rs. They name the patient in each search.
  • Ask for launch/patient patient/*.rs.
  • Sign in as josh, sam-caregiver or robin. No password.
  • Discovery at your FHIR address names the imaging server.
