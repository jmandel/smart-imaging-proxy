Build a client

Your SMART app, against this sandbox's servers. They behave as a health system's would, with sample patients, real policy decisions, and sign-in with no passwords.

1 Register your app

A public app, a confidential app with a key, or a backend service. Registering takes a name and a redirect URI or a key, and gives you a client ID.

2 Point it at this sandbox

FHIR base

Discovery
{{SITE}}/fhir/.well-known/smart-configuration
Imaging FHIR server
{{SITE}}/imaging/fhir
  • Apps use PKCE (S256). Confidential apps also send a private_key_jwt assertion, signed with ES256, ES384 or RS384.
  • Ask for launch/patient patient/*.rs. Backend services use client_credentials with system/ImagingStudy.rs, and name the patient in each search.
  • Discovery names the imaging FHIR server above in associated_endpoints. In Settings, the sandbox can instead be one server, with imaging at the FHIR base.
